Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how this application ("the App") handles your Google account and Google Drive data.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects the following data:

  • Google account authentication information (OAuth token)
  • File metadata and contents that you choose to upload to or download from Google Drive via the App

2. How We Use the Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To allow you to upload to and download from your Google Drive
  • To authenticate and authorize your access to Google Drive services

3. Data Storage and Sharing

The App does not store your Google Drive data on any external servers.
OAuth tokens and any user data are not shared with third parties.

4. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect user data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
